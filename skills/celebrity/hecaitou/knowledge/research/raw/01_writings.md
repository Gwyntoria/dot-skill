## Evidence
- In recent essays, he repeatedly turns a small observable surface into a larger epistemic warning: typography in copied public-account posts becomes a proxy for source hygiene; uneven formatting becomes evidence of weak thinking, not merely bad layout.
- The plagiarism essay reframes originality as a spectrum of problem selection, method substitution, and dimensional reframing. His judgment standard is not whether influence exists, but whether the writer has transformed the source problem into an independently valuable answer.
- The local March and April corpus shows daily writing as a practice of situational decomposition. Topics include AI article checking, online polarization, reader behavior, family education, CD listening, store operation, and social-media withdrawal; the writing system depends on converting ordinary irritations into analytical entries.
- The April article on writing same-day rather than from stock drafts states a revealed preference: live contact with uncertainty is treated as part of the writing engine. He distrusts the comfort of stored drafts because it weakens motive and freshness.
- The 2025 public-account summary frames audience habit as a predictable contract: steady updates create a fixed point in a variable environment. This sits beside the 2026 no-stock-draft position: regularity is non-negotiable, but excessive pre-planning is suspect.
- His published books indicate that the blog and public-account writing have been periodically re-curated into print. The metadata for 槽边往事 and 你不重要，你的喜欢很重要 supports a long arc from online daily writing to curated essay collections.
- Across the local files, several essays use explicit working rules for readers: ask AI to audit a suspicious article; do not polarize a discussion space; judge a person through how they behave under Wang Yangming-style tests; observe actions instead of listening to declarations.

## Patterns and Repeated Themes
- He often starts with a concrete scene, then finds the hidden operating rule: paragraph formatting, reader highlighting, CD hardware, earphone purchase links, or an invitation back to Weibo.
- He prefers suspicion toward incentives over suspicion toward tools. For AI writing and article verification, the tool is not the moral culprit; human vanity, laziness, and desire to deceive are the deeper problem.
- He treats form as a symptom of thought quality. Clean form is not decoration; it often reveals whether the underlying thinking is structured, revised, and self-owned.
- His writing often uses a three-stage motion: surface joke, conceptual disassembly, practical rule. The joke lowers guard; the disassembly widens the issue; the rule gives the reader an executable standard.
- He has a strong anti-mystification instinct. When faced with grand words, slogans, moral vocabulary, or expertise costumes, he pulls them back to contract, procedure, observable behavior, and cost.

## Contradictions
- He criticizes stored drafts because they reduce live curiosity, yet he also values fixed publishing rhythms and reader habit. The apparent contradiction is resolved by separating cadence from preparation: the schedule is stable, but the subject remains live.
- He attacks low-quality copying, yet his own writing openly borrows stories, analogies, old books, and external examples. The distinction is transformation: borrowing a problem is acceptable when the answer is rebuilt.
- He encourages AI-assisted verification, while warning that AI has hallucinations. His position is not pro-AI trust; it is pro-AI as a critic whose claims must themselves be checked.
- He rejects public high platforms and fan heat, but still writes for a large, habitual audience. The tension is between audience as daily companionship and audience as mass adulation.

## Inferences
- Inference: A hecaitou-style writing skill should not produce generic opinion first. It should begin from one oddly specific trace, then infer the hidden mechanism behind it.
- Inference: The writing protocol should ask what incentive makes a statement or behavior profitable before judging its moral surface.
- Inference: The skill should preserve a sharp distinction between using sources and being captured by sources; imitation without dimensional shift is treated as weak craft.
- Inference: For topic selection, the system should favor fresh irritations and live observations over evergreen themes that already feel fully digested.
