## Evidence
- Across the local 2025-06 to 2026-05 corpus, the writing repeatedly starts from ordinary objects or scenes, then expands into a social, platform, or mind-training model. Earphones, compact discs, flowers, platform metrics, reader comments, job vocabulary, and family choices function as entry points rather than final subjects.
- In the essay on reader behavior, writing is framed as an online existence practice: what a person writes and how continuously they write becomes the online identity others can recognize. This is first-person craft theory, not only commentary about audience growth.
- In the description-first essay, he treats narration as a basic social competence. Clear description proves observation and thought before it proves literary style.
- In the reading-horse-stance sequence, reading is not sorted by school-subject categories. The primary axis is whether a book expands curiosity and builds a mental map of the world.
- In the AI editing essay, he accepts machine editing as useful for surface repair but resists over-smoothing, because the irregularity of a living voice carries the personality of the text.
- The public blog restart essay shows a platform theory: blogs, unlike closed app feeds, support revision, search, linking, and durable long-form context.
- The book metadata pages establish that the 2015/2016 self-selected essay collection was marketed as a distillation of years of online writing, with readers and publishers treating him as a long-running internet essayist rather than a one-platform columnist.

## Patterns and Repeated Themes
- A small concrete thing is used as an interface to a larger system: product interfaces lead to platform power, shopping leads to contract ethics, reader comments lead to literacy and attention.
- He prefers process disclosure to compressed wisdom. The route by which a judgment is reached matters more than the quotable conclusion.
- He treats writing as a relationship with readers, but not as reader service. Readers are participants, feedback sources, and sometimes discipline problems.
- Craft advice often appears as anti-craft advice: do not start by chasing style, do not worship inspiration, do not let AI polish away the odd corners.
- Long-term writing is framed as stamina and daily existence, not as occasional genius.

## Contradictions
- He resists authority and expert posture, yet his essays often issue strong directives about work, family, platforms, and reading.
- He criticizes platform metrics and audience capture, yet he has built a recognizably durable public self through repeated publication on platforms.
- He values irregularity and personal bias in writing, yet he also cares about clarity, description, factual discipline, and reader comprehension.
- He positions writing as ordinary daily work, yet external reception and publication metadata treat the same work as representative contemporary online prose.

## Inferences (clearly marked)
- Inference: His core writing skill is not topic selection but conversion, turning a daily object into a model of human conduct or platform behavior.
- Inference: The apparent looseness of the prose is controlled; digressions are used as breathing spaces and as proof that the speaker is alive rather than institutionally optimized.
- Inference: He would reject a writing skill that only imitates jokes or sentence rhythm; the deeper rule is to preserve the route of observation.
- Inference: His craft theory privileges reader attention without surrendering to reader convenience.
