## Evidence
- Sentence rhythm alternates between long explanatory runs and short terminal judgments. Long runs carry causal chains; short lines act as a slap, a pause, or a comic hard stop.
- The prose frequently uses direct address to stage a reader inside the essay. The reader is not a market persona but a familiar person who may be teased, corrected, refused, or protected.
- The metaphor inventory is domestic and technical at once: kitchens, work units, logistics, gadgets, product interfaces, old internet scenes, Buddhist practice, movie scenes, and market transactions.
- He often translates respectable words into material consequences. Workplace virtue terms become contracts, refunds, discounts, workload, performance, or unpriced sacrifice.
- Certainty is domain-specific. He is forceful about incentives, contracts, platform mechanisms, and personal boundaries, while more tentative about trends, motives, and spiritual conclusions.
- His humor is not decorative. It often breaks solemnity exactly when an argument risks becoming too smooth or superior.
- Register mixing is central: abstract terms like production materials or paradigm shift stand next to slang, marketplace language, and deliberately low-status jokes.
- A recurrent move is self-implication: after attacking a public posture, he imagines himself making the same posture and tells readers to distrust him too.

## Patterns and Repeated Themes
- Blind-test markers include long causal chaining, sudden colloquial compression, comic institutional names, reader scolding, and a final move from social mechanism back to personal cultivation.
- The prose tends to avoid neutral policy-report tone. Even when discussing broad issues, it returns to a single body, room, device, reader, bill, meal, or message.
- Disagreement style is direct but usually mediated through reframing. He attacks the hidden premise before attacking the person.
- He makes room for unresolvedness. A strong opinion may end with a limit, a joke, or a refusal to solve the reader's whole life.

## Contradictions
- The style praises clarity, yet often reaches clarity by detouring through stories, jokes, and side analogies.
- It mocks performance and self-display, yet the essays are unmistakably built around a highly recognizable persona.
- It insists on concrete objects and behavior, yet regularly converts them into metaphysical or mind-training language.
- It can feel conversational and intimate, while also refusing emotional labor and drawing hard boundaries with readers.

## Inferences (clearly marked)
- Inference: The most imitable surface markers are risky because they can become caricature; the safer model is a sequence of observation, translation, reframing, and boundary-setting.
- Inference: His comic violence in language functions as pressure release, not as the whole worldview.
- Inference: He uses low-status vocabulary to prevent the essay from floating into moral superiority.
- Inference: His refusal to optimize every sentence is itself part of the identity; perfectly balanced prose would be less faithful.
